Sakit lutut? Mungkin ini puncanya

Knee pain · 3 min

Pain at the front of the knee often comes from the quadriceps and the patellar tendon, not the joint itself.

Read the full tip

Injury, overuse or repeated pressure on the quadriceps and patellar tendon can cause pain at the front of the knee and inflammation at the tibial tuberosity, the bump below the kneecap. A full assessment tells us which structure is the problem. Treatment usually combines physiotherapy, strengthening and a gradual return to activity, so you can get back to moving without pain.

Sport massage or physiotherapy?

Choosing treatment · 2 min

Tired, tight muscles? A massage helps. Pain that stops you moving? That needs a physiotherapist.

Read the full tip

Sport massage relaxes tight muscles and helps recovery after training. Physiotherapy starts with a diagnosis, then treats the cause with manual therapy, electrotherapy and exercise. If pain lasts longer than a few days, keeps coming back, or comes with swelling, numbness or weakness, see a physiotherapist first.

Stroke recovery: one percent a day

Stroke rehab · 3 min

Recovery after a stroke is physical, emotional and mental. It is not about speed. It is about keeping going.

Read the full tip

The brain keeps re-learning for months after a stroke, especially with regular, repeated practice. Small daily goals, like standing a little longer or lifting a cup, add up. Families make a big difference: we teach you how to help safely at home between sessions.

What is IFT?

Electrotherapy · 2 min

Interferential therapy uses a gentle electrical current to ease muscle tightness and pain.

Read the full tip

Two small currents cross under the skin, creating a soft tingling deep in the muscle. Most people find it relaxing. IFT does not fix the cause on its own, but by reducing pain it makes the exercises that do fix it much easier.

Shockwave therapy: who it helps

Shockwave · 2 min

Good for tight muscles and trigger points, tendon problems, plantar fasciitis and chronic soft tissue pain.

Read the full tip

Shockwave sends pulses of sound energy into the tissue to restart healing in problems that have become stuck. Sessions are short, usually once a week, and many people notice a difference within three to five sessions.

Perut kembung? Cuba bekam perut

Bloating · 2 min

Bloating, a full feeling and discomfort after meals: abdominal cupping can bring relief.

Read the full tip

Bekam perut uses gentle suction cups on the abdomen to relax the muscles and help release trapped wind. If bloating comes with weight loss, blood or severe pain, see a doctor first.
