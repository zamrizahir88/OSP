How it works

Your path from pain to performance

Four clear steps, explained before we start, so you always know where you are.

Your first visit
  1. 1

    Assess

    We listen, test how you move and find what is really causing the pain.

  2. 2

    Treat

    Hands-on therapy, IFT, shockwave or cupping to bring the pain down.

  3. 3

    Rebuild

    Exercises in our gym and a home plan, so the problem does not come back.

  4. 4

    Perform

    Back to work, to sport and to daily life, stronger than before.

FAQ

Before you come

Quick answers to what people ask us most. Still unsure? Message us.

All questions
Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book with us directly. If you have a doctor's letter, X-ray, MRI or surgery report, please bring it to your first visit.

How much does a session cost?

It depends on what we find and what you need, so we assess you first and tell you the price before treatment starts. Current offers are on our promotions page.

Are you open on Friday?

No, we are closed every Friday. We are open Saturday to Thursday, 9am to 6pm.

Do you treat patients at home?

Yes. Our home physio service covers Changlun and nearby towns, mostly for stroke patients, older people and anyone just home from surgery. How home physio works.

Can I ask for a female physiotherapist?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will arrange it.
