Conditions we treat

If it hurts when you move, or you cannot move the way you used to, we can probably help. Here is what we see most.

Try the body map

Spine and joints

The commonest reasons people walk through our door.

Slip disc and sciatica

Back pain that can shoot down one leg, with numbness or pins and needles. We calm the nerve first, then strengthen the back so it holds.

Neck pain and stiffness

Often from long hours on a phone or laptop. Manual therapy, IFT and posture exercises.

Frozen shoulder

A shoulder that slowly stiffens until you cannot reach behind your back. Recovery takes patience, and the right stretches speed it up.

Knee pain and osteoarthritis

Pain on stairs, stiffness after sitting, swelling. Strength work and joint care help most people avoid or delay surgery.

Muscle and joint pain

General aches, tight muscles and joints that click or catch. We find out why before we treat.

Post-operation rehabilitation

After knee or hip replacement, ligament repair, fractures and spinal surgery.

Brain and nerves

Longer programmes, at the centre or at home, with the family involved.

Stroke rehabilitation

Getting back walking, balance, and the use of an arm and hand. Recovery is not about speed. One percent a day still counts.

Bell's palsy

Sudden weakness on one side of the face. Early facial exercises and stimulation help the muscles recover.

Parkinson's disease

Exercises for walking, balance and stiffness, to keep you independent for longer.

Balance and walking problems

Unsteadiness, fear of falling, or needing a stick. We train strength and balance safely between parallel bars.

Sport and soft tissue

For UUM students, athletes and anyone who trains.

ACL and meniscus injury

From the first week after injury or surgery to running and jumping again.

Muscle strains and sprains

Hamstrings, calves, groin and ankles, with a clear plan back to play.

Plantar fasciitis

Sharp heel pain on your first steps in the morning. Shockwave and stretching work well together.

Tendon problems

Achilles, patellar and elbow tendons that ache with use. Loading exercises and shockwave.

Bloating and wind

Perut kembung and discomfort after meals. Bekam perut is one of our most requested treatments.
