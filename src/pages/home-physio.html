Who it is for

Home visits are best when travel would tire you out or put you at risk.

  • After a stroke Walking, transfers, arm and hand work in the place you actually live.
  • Bedridden patients Chest care, positioning, and exercises to prevent stiffness and sores.
  • Older people Strength and balance to stay safe at home and prevent falls.
  • Just home from surgery Knee or hip replacement and fracture rehab in the first weeks.

Where we go

From our centre in Changlun across northern Kedah. Travel charges depend on distance.
