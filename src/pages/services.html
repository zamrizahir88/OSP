Sports injury rehabilitation

Students, athletes, weekend players

Strains, sprains, ligament and tendon injuries, ACL and meniscus rehab, and testing before you return to play.

Stroke and neurological rehab

At the centre or at home

Stroke, Bell's palsy and Parkinson's disease. Walking, balance, arm and hand function, and training for the family.

Spine and joint pain

Back, neck, shoulder, knee

Slip disc, sciatica, frozen shoulder and knee osteoarthritis, treated with manual therapy, IFT and exercise.

Post-operation rehabilitation

After surgery or a fracture

Knee and hip replacements, ligament repairs and fractures. We work to your surgeon's protocol.

Shockwave therapy

Often 3 to 5 sessions

For plantar fasciitis, tendon problems, trigger points and chronic soft tissue pain.

Interferential therapy (IFT)

Part of most pain programmes

Gentle electrical current that eases muscle tightness and pain, so exercise becomes possible.

Paediatric and postnatal care

Mothers and children

Recovery after childbirth, back and pelvic pain, and physiotherapy for children's movement problems.
