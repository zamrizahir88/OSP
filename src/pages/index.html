2015

the year OSP was registered in Changlun

One Stop Physiotherapy is a physiotherapy centre in Taman Sena, Changlun, a short drive from UUM. It was started by Hajar D'zohir, a certified physiotherapist, and her husband Zhafri Zakaria, who runs the business. The idea is in the name: one place for every stage of getting better. Come in with a fresh sports injury, a back that has hurt for years or a parent recovering from a stroke, and leave with a plan. We assess first, treat with hands-on therapy, IFT and shockwave, then rebuild strength in our exercise room. For recovery we also offer massage, cupping and hot blanket therapy.

How recovery goes

Assess

We listen, test how you move and find what is really causing the pain.

Treat

Hands-on therapy, IFT, shockwave or cupping to bring the pain down.

Rebuild

Exercises in our gym and a plan for home, so it does not come back.

Perform

Back to work, to the field, to carrying your children without thinking about it.

Before you come

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book with us directly. If you have a doctor's letter, X-ray, MRI or surgery report, please bring it to your first visit.

How much does a session cost?

It depends on what we find and what you need, so we assess you first and tell you the price before any treatment starts. Current offers are on our promotions page.

Are you open on Friday?

No, we are closed every Friday. We are open Saturday to Thursday, 9am to 6pm.

Do you treat patients at home?

Yes. Our home physio service covers Changlun and nearby towns, mostly for stroke patients, older people and anyone just home from surgery. How home physio works.

Can I ask for a female physiotherapist?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will arrange it.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothes. For knee or ankle problems, bring shorts or trousers that roll up above the knee.
