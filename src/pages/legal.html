Privacy and policies

How we handle your information, our appointment policy and the small print.

Privacy notice

One Stop Physiotherapy Centre (201503138087) respects your privacy and handles personal data under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and IC number when you register
  • Medical history, assessment notes and treatment records
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Facebook or TikTok

How we use it

Only to book appointments, treat you, keep clinical records and contact you about your care. We do not sell or share your data, except with your consent or where the law requires it.

This website

The forms on this website do not store your details. They open WhatsApp with your message, and you choose whether to send it. Embedded maps and TikTok videos load only when shown or tapped, and are covered by Google's and TikTok's own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you by contacting us on 012-413 7401.

Appointments and cancellation

  • Please arrive ten minutes before your appointment.
  • If you cannot come, tell us at least 24 hours before.
  • If you arrive late, your session may be shorter so the next patient is not kept waiting.
  • Promotional prices apply only on the stated dates and while slots last.

Medical disclaimer

The information on this website is general and does not replace a personal assessment. If you have severe pain, chest pain, sudden weakness, numbness or loss of bladder or bowel control, go to the nearest hospital immediately.
