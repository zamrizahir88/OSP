Your first visit

No surprises. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you message us.

What happens

Plan for about an hour on your first visit.

  1. Book

    Message us on WhatsApp

    Tell us your name, what hurts and when you are free. We confirm a time, usually the same or next day.

  2. Arrive

    Come ten minutes early

    Our front desk takes your details and any reports or scans you bring.

  3. Talk

    Tell us your story

    When it started, what makes it better or worse, and what you want to get back to doing.

  4. Test

    Movement and strength tests

    Your physiotherapist checks how you move, where it hurts and what is weak or tight.

  5. Plan

    Diagnosis, plan and price

    We explain what is wrong in plain words, how many sessions it should take and what it costs. You decide.

  6. Start

    First treatment and exercises

    Most people get their first treatment the same day, and leave with exercises to do at home.

Good to know

What to wear

Loose, comfortable clothes. Shorts or rollable trousers for knee, ankle or hip problems. A sleeveless top under your clothes helps for shoulder and neck problems.

What to bring

  • Your IC
  • Any doctor's letter, X-ray, MRI or surgery report
  • A list of the medicines you take

Female physiotherapist

Available on request. Let us know when you book.

Payment

Cash, online transfer and DuitNow QR. Payment methods and panel or insurance coverage to confirm

Parking

Free parking in front of the shop lots on Susuran Sena.

Changing or cancelling

Please WhatsApp us at least 24 hours before if you cannot make it, so we can offer the slot to someone else.
