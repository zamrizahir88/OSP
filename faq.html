Booking and visits

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book with us directly. If you have a doctor's letter, X-ray, MRI or surgery report, please bring it.

When are you open?

Saturday to Thursday, 9am to 6pm. We are closed every Friday.

How do I book?

WhatsApp 012-407 7401 or call 012-413 7401. You can also use the form on our contact page.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

You can, but we may be fully booked. A quick WhatsApp first saves you a wasted trip.

How long is a session?

Plan for about an hour on your first visit. Follow-up sessions are usually 30 to 45 minutes.

Can I ask for a female physiotherapist?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will arrange it.

Can I cancel or change my appointment?

Yes. Please let us know at least 24 hours before, so we can offer your slot to someone else.

Treatment and prices

How much does physiotherapy cost?

Prices depend on your condition and treatment plan, so we assess you first and tell you the price before any treatment starts. Massage and cupping prices are on our services page.

How many sessions will I need?

Many problems improve in four to eight sessions. Stroke and nerve conditions need longer programmes. We give you an honest estimate after the assessment.

Does it hurt?

Some treatments and exercises can be uncomfortable, but we always work within what you can manage, and we explain everything before we do it.

What is the difference between massage and physiotherapy?

Massage relaxes muscles and helps recovery. Physiotherapy diagnoses the cause of pain or weakness and treats it with manual therapy, equipment and exercise. Read more in our health tips.

Is cupping safe?

Our therapists use clean, single-use materials for bekam darah. Tell us if you are pregnant, take blood thinners or have a bleeding disorder.

How can I pay?

Cash, online transfer and DuitNow QR.
