Our founders

A physiotherapist and a director, married, building one clinic in Changlun since 2015.

Hajar D'zohir

Physiotherapist and co-founder

Assalamualaikum, and welcome to One Stop Physiotherapy.

I became a physiotherapist because I wanted to help people move again, not just feel better for a day. Too many people around Changlun travel far for treatment, or give up and live with the pain. OSP is our answer: one place where you are assessed properly, treated with the right equipment and guided until you are strong again.

Every patient gets the same three things from us: time to be listened to, an honest explanation of what is wrong, and a plan that fits their life. A UUM student with a sports injury, a mother after giving birth, a family caring for someone after a stroke: we walk the road with you.

From pain to performance is not a slogan for us. It is the promise we make to every patient.

Hajar D'zohir

Zhafri Zakaria

Director and co-founder

When Hajar and I started OSP, the plan was simple: give Changlun a physiotherapy centre people would be proud to recommend.

I look after the business so our physiotherapists can focus on patients. That means investing in equipment like IFT and shockwave, keeping prices fair with regular promotions, and growing a team who share our values.

I also spend a lot of time out in the community, with schools, sports teams and youth groups across Kedah, because the best treatment is the injury that never happens.

We are still a family business, and we still treat every patient like family. Thank you for trusting us.

Zhafri Zakaria

How OSP grew

From one treatment room to a full centre in Taman Sena.

Dates and details to confirm with the founders

  1. 2015

    OSP is registered

    One Stop Physiotherapy Centre is registered in Kedah (201503138087) with one goal: proper physiotherapy, close to home.

  2. Taman Sena

    A home in Changlun

    The centre settles at No 5A Susuran Sena, with a reception, private treatment rooms and an exercise room.

  3. Equipment

    IFT and shockwave

    New electrotherapy and shockwave machines let the team treat tendon, nerve and chronic muscle pain faster.

  4. Outreach

    Home visits and talks

    Home physio for stroke and bedridden patients, plus talks on back pain and slip disc for companies and schools.

  5. 2026

    A growing community

    More than 6,400 people follow OSP on Facebook, and the team shares real sessions on TikTok every week.

What we promise

Four things every patient can expect, from the first visit to the last.

  • Listen first Your story comes before any machine or massage.
  • Explain honestly You will know what is wrong, how long it should take and what it will cost.
  • Treat the cause Pain relief is the start. Fixing why it hurts is the job.
  • Stay with you Exercises for home, follow-up on WhatsApp, and a plan until you are back.
