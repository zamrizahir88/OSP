Sports and corporate

We take physiotherapy out of the clinic: to the field, the office and the lecture hall.

Ask about a programme

For sports teams

School teams, university clubs and community leagues across Kedah.

Pre-season screening

Movement and strength tests for every player, to spot injury risks before the season starts.

Pitch-side support

A physiotherapist at matches and tournaments for taping, first aid and injury assessment.

Injury rehab and return to play

Priority appointments for team players, and clear guidance for coaches on when a player is ready.

Recovery sessions

Sports massage and recovery after heavy training blocks.

For companies and schools

Talks and workshops in Malay or English, at your premises.

Back pain and slip disc talks

What a slip disc is, why it happens and how to prevent it, with simple exercises staff can do at their desks.

Ergonomics at work

Desk set-up, lifting and posture, for office and warehouse teams.

Health day screening

Posture and pain screening booths for company health days and school events.
